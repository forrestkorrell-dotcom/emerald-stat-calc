Recommended Team

Pokémon Type Role
Swampert Water / Ground Physical tank, early Gym sweeper
Breloom Grass / Fighting Physical attacker
Gardevoir Psychic Special sweeper
Manectric Electric Fast special attacker
Skarmory Flying / Steel Flying, Tank
Camerupt Fire / Ground Mixed Special attacker

Swampert: Evolves from Mudkip, generally understood to be the best starter due to it's good stats, resilient typing once evolved, and good matchups early game.

Breloom: Takes care of the many water types present in Emerald and has a versatile moveset with access to sleep and life gain. It also does well against late-game ice and steel types in important battles.

Gardevoir: Ralts can be found early game with some persistence, and once evolved psychic attacks buffed with Calm Mind will one-shot many opponents.

Manectric: Extra coverage for water types and late game steel. Jolteon has better speed but is slightly less bulky. This slot is flexible if you are willing to ditch electric.

Skarmory: Takes fly which when combined with toxic and other defensive moves can make Skarmory an incredible tank which can outlast even opponents which it is poorly matched for.

Camerupt: Not many fire type options in emerald, but your Swampert will thank you for having something to take care of grass types, and Camerupt has surprisingly versatile move access.

Or!

Any team that you enjoy! Most pokemon can be made viable if played well and in a team with a good type spread. Use whatever you like the most!
