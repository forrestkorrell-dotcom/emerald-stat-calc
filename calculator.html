Gen 3 Stat Calculator

A Pokémon's stats are based on hidden IVs (random, 0–31, set at generation), EVs (earned through battling, up to 252 per stat), and Nature (boosts one stat 10%, lowers one 10%). Combined with base stats and level, these determine true stats. Use the calculator below to plug in your own values and see these resulting stats.
